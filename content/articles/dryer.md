# The Dryer Is a Casino

The clothes dryer is a casino. This is not a metaphor; the building codes of 1947 classify residential dryers as &lsquo;games of chance with heating elements.&rsquo; Socks are the chips. The spin cycle is the roulette wheel. The lint trap is the house take, skimmed quietly off every load since the Truman administration.

A missing sock has not been lost. It has been lost <i>at roulette</i>, and the distinction matters to the house. Winners are paid in static cling, a currency with no exchange rate and infinite circulation. The dryer sheets are not softening anything; they are the complimentary drinks, and they are working exactly as intended.

> “The house always wins. The house is warm. The house thanks you..” — Pit Boss of Lint, posted on the wall, in the laundry room
