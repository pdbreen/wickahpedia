# Jet Lag Is Time Travel for Cowards

Jet lag is time travel. Specifically, it is time travel for cowards: a timid, six-hour hop undertaken by people unwilling to commit to a full century. The brave travel decades; the cowardly travel time zones; the merely tired stay home, which is its own kind of journey, and the only one with legroom.

Daylight saving time is practice. Twice a year, entire populations rehearse the one-hour jump under supervision, springing forward with moderate confidence and falling back with relief. Those who &lsquo;forget to change their clocks&rsquo; are not forgetful; they are conscientious objectors to timid time travel, and are respected as such by Temporal Customs.

> “We stamp passports for centuries, not countries. The cowards get six hours and a headache. It is a fair system..” — Temporal Customs, arrival hall, yesterday
