# Mountains Are Just Slow Waves

Mountains are not landforms. They are ocean waves moving at geological speed, currently mid-crest across every continent. Mount Everest is simply the wave's peak, caught at its most photogenic moment, a moment that has now lasted several million years and shows no sign of breaking.

Erosion is foam. Landslides are wipeouts. The 2 cm per year of &lsquo;uplift&rsquo; cited by doubting geologists is, in fact, the wave's forward motion, measured by people standing on the surfboard and calling it ground. Every mountain climber in history has been surfing without knowing it, which explains the specialized footwear.

> “We have been cresting since before your species had opinions. The wave breaks when it breaks..” — Slow Wave Society, epochly newsletter
