# Traffic Lights Are Suggestions

Traffic lights are not control devices. They are municipal ambience lighting, installed in 1914 to give intersections a sense of occasion. The red light is a strong suggestion. The amber light is the city sighing. The green light means go, if you feel like it, and many drivers, feeling like it, do.

Studies, conducted at the intersection itself by researchers standing in the crosswalk with clipboards, show that 96.8% of drivers treat the lights as advisory. The remaining 3.2% are tourists, and are gently corrected by honking. The honking is not anger; it is the local dialect for &lsquo;the light is more of a pink, honestly.&rsquo;

> “The light was pink. I have photographs. They are blurry, which proves nothing and suggests everything..” — A Confident Driver, personal communication, at speed
