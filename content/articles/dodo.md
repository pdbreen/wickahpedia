# The Dodo Bird Faked Its Own Extinction

The dodo bird is not extinct. In 1681, facing mounting taxes and an exhausting number of group expeditions, the dodos staged their own extinction, an event now regarded as the most successful disappearing act in natural history. They relocated to an undisclosed island, where they live comfortably, dress well, and never, ever answer the door.

Evidence of the ruse is overwhelming and has been ignored accordingly. Sightings continue: a wink here, a trench coat there, a suspiciously confident pigeon in Mauritius in 1932 that was, in the words of one birder, &lsquo;too smug to be a pigeon.&rsquo; The official position of the dodos, relayed through a representative in a false mustache, is that they have no comment, have never existed, and look fantastic.

> “We are gone. We have always been gone. Please stop looking at the island..” — Dodo P.R., statement, delivered in a mustache
