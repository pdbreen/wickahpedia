# Lighthouses Are Just Jealous Candles

Lighthouses are jealous of candles. The candle sits on a table, close to people, warm and intimate, while the lighthouse stands alone on a rock, flashing desperately at ships that never write back. The great rotating beam is not a navigational aid; it is showing off, and the ships know it.

The foghorn is a sigh, amplified through a brass throat the size of a doorway. Keepers log each sounding with notes like &lsquo;extra tonight&rsquo; and &lsquo;the candle was mentioned on the radio.&rsquo; Structural engineers have confirmed the jealousy is load-bearing: remove it, and several lighthouses would simply lie down.

> “No..” — The Candle, the entire comment, fortieth year
