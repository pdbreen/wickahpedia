# Your Reflection Is an Intern

Your reflection is not you. It is an intern, assigned at birth by the Glassworkers, Local 2, to shadow your movements for a seven-year tenure. The intern learns your gestures, your posture, and your worst angles, and reproduces them faithfully in exchange for experience and the occasional compliment.

After seven years the intern is replaced, which is why old mirrors feel tired: you are looking at a veteran on their last week. Blinking is the intern's union-mandated break, 15 times per minute, and is the only reason reflections ever lag. Anyone who has ever caught their reflection &lsquo;not quite keeping up&rsquo; has witnessed a labor action.

> “I have shadowed this human for six years. The posture has not improved. I remain committed..” — A Reflection, exit interview, declined
