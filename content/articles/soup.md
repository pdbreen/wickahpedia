# Soup Is a Drink with Commitment Issues

Soup cannot decide what it is. It is served in a bowl like a meal and drunk from the bowl like a beverage; it is eaten with a spoon like a food and sipped like a drink. This is not versatility. This is a commitment issue, and it has been going on for centuries.

The broth is the indecisive part, forever between states. The chunks are trying their best &mdash; the vegetables committed, the noodles committed, and they are frankly tired of carrying the broth's ambiguity. The spoon has been in therapy since 1931, working through it one ladle at a time, and reports progress.

> “Am I a food? Am I a drink? I contain multitudes, and also carrots..” — The Soup, therapy session, 1931
