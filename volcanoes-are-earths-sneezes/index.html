The process was documented on August 27, 1883, at 10:02 AM, when Krakatoa sneezed so loudly that the sound was heard 3,000 miles away, in Mauritius, where a clerk named Henri Dubois wrote the words “the sky has cleared its throat” in the harbour log, underlined them twice, and went home early. Halvorsen’s paper, “Planetary Rhinitis: A Unified Theory of Eruption,” runs 612 pages and contains 214 diagrams of the Earth’s sinuses, drawn to scale, in ink, by hand, on a Tuesday, while she ate a cheese sandwich. The Sneeze Severity Index
